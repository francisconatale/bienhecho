# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Freelancers and small business owners managing their budgets, clients, and current accounts.

## Product Purpose
A reliable and fast CRM/Invoicing tool to track client jobs, statuses, and financial balances, providing a streamlined workflow for managing daily operations.

## Brand Personality
Professional, modern, reliable, and tactile.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI SaaS templates (flat, cream-colored, heavily card-based).
- Mobile-stretched layouts on desktop.
- Monotonous lists without clear visual hierarchy.

## Design Principles
- **Premium Tactility**: Use depth, glassmorphism, and colored shadows deliberately, avoiding flat utility-first styling.
- **Master-Detail Flow**: Maintain context without losing focus.
- **In-place Editing**: Read-only by default, seamless editing in context without disruptive modals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Clear contrast ratios for all critical text.
- Reduced motion alternatives for all animations.
